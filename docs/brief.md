**For:** indie SaaS and software founders selling subscriptions on Lemon Squeezy who have PayPal subscribers (or customers on India-issued cards who must reauthorise).

## Answers to common questions

- **Can't Lemon Squeezy's tool do this?** It moves card subscriptions. PayPal subscriptions can't migrate; customers must repurchase. It doesn't give each one a personal checkout, chase them, cancel the old sub, or track who came back.
- **Will customers be charged twice?** No. The new subscription's first charge is the old renewal date; the old one is cancelled at period end once the new one is active.
- **What access do you need?** A fresh Lemon Squeezy API key (revoke after) and a Stripe restricted key or Polar token. Card details go only into Stripe/Polar checkout. We store names, emails, plan and dates, deleted 30 days after the campaign.
- **What if a customer ignores the emails?** They stay on Lemon Squeezy until March 31; the dashboard lists them for personal follow-up.
- **Which providers?** v1: Stripe and Polar. Next: Paddle, Creem, Dodo if founding stores ask.
- **License keys and downloads?** Out of scope; Keypost (open source) and Polar cover those.
- **Why one-time pricing?** It's a one-off job that ends March 31, 2027.
